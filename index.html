        Sophia Librarian will help you draft polished, professional outgoing
        emails for quotes and order confirmations. Pre-fills with the customer's
        name, items, dates, and totals — then runs the draft through an AI polish
        pass before you copy it into your email client.
      

    

    

      

        Quote Email
        Friendly intro + summary of items quoted + clear call to action with the
        next step (review, sign, deposit).
      

      

        Order Confirmation
        Booked-and-confirmed tone, restates event date + delivery / pickup details
        + reminders for what the customer needs to know day-of.
      

      

        Tone Match
        Sounds like a coordinator, not a chatbot. Warm, capable, professional —
        whichever brand voice fits the record.
      

      

        Editable Output
        Final draft sits in an editor for last-minute tweaks before you copy it
        into Gmail / Outlook / wherever you actually send from.
